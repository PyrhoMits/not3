# Governance

Not3 has a single lead maintainer ([@PyrhoMits](https://github.com/PyrhoMits)), who has the final say on direction and merges. Day-to-day decisions happen in issues and pull requests: discuss, aim for consensus, and the maintainer decides when there is none. Existing decisions are recorded in [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) and `ROADMAP.md`; open an issue to propose changing one.

Fixed principles: free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later), no premium features, offline-first, no analytics or third-party tracking.

Regular contributors may be invited as maintainers (review and merge rights) after sustained, good-quality contributions. Maintainers follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
