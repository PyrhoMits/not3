# Support

- **How do I…?** Read the [README](README.md) and [`docs/testing.md`](docs/testing.md). Then open a [Discussion or issue](https://github.com/PyrhoMits/not3/issues/new/choose).
- **Found a bug?** Open an issue with the bug template. In *Settings → Error log* you can copy the local log and attach it; it never leaves your device unless you share it.
- **Security:** see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
- **Want to help?** See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Not3 is maintained by volunteers; replies may take a while.
